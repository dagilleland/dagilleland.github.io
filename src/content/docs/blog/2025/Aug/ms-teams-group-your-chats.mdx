Microsoft Teams is continually improving. Now, in 2025, you can move your chats to custom groups (called "sections"). Here's how.

<Steps>
1. Open MS Teams and list your chats.
1. Hover over the person/chat you want to move. Click the ellipsis called "More options".

    <Image src={step1} alt="Step 1" />

1. Under the "Move to >" option, select the existing section or create a new section.

    <Image src={step2} alt="Step 2" />

</Steps>

It's that simple! Now, you can better organize your chats in whatever way suits you the best. Enjoy!
