Over the centuries, people have rejected Jesus because they looked at the Church and saw hipocrites mired in sin and/or legalists berift of love and mercy.

Why is the Church so often messed up in this way?

- One of the reasons we have problems with sin in the Church is that we don't teach enough about repentance unto good works.
- One of the reasons we have problems with love in the Church is that we don't teach enough about salvation being by grace alone through faith alone in Christ alone.
